# Sky's Grass Slabs 1.2.1 for Minecraft 1.21.1

Slabs beside a higher snow-covered terrace now keep their snowy top.
They also recognise the snow pieces supplied by Sky's Building Pieces.
Exposed slabs in cold areas keep a snow cap even where a snow layer cannot
settle. In warmer areas, removing the surrounding snow restores their green
or dirt appearance.

Different crafting results now have separate recipe-book buttons. Slab joins
also respect placement permissions and leave room for players and creatures.

This build uses Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.247 and Java 21.
