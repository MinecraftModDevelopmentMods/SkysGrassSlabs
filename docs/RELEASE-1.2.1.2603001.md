# Sky's Grass Slabs 1.2.1 for Forge 26.3

Grass and dirt slabs pick up snow from nearby higher terraces and exposed cold
ground. Different crafting results have their own recipe-book buttons, so the
book no longer cycles between unrelated shapes.

Slabs cannot combine into a full block while an entity occupies the space. Turf
also respects the player's building permissions when greening a dirt slab.

Existing blocks, items, settings and saved worlds keep their identities. Terrain
Smoother remains optional; Grass Slabs still works on its own.
