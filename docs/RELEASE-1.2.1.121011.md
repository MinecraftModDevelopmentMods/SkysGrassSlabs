# Sky’s Grass Slabs 1.2.1 for Forge 1.21.1

Grass and dirt slabs now take their snow appearance from nearby snowy slopes as
soon as they are placed. Grass still spreads between supported grass blocks,
slabs and turf, and covered slabs turn back into dirt without changing their
orientation.

Different crafting results have separate recipe-book buttons. Slab joining also
respects entity collision, and trees decorating new chunks leave existing saved
slabs alone.

Existing block IDs, configuration, recipes and saved migration reports are
preserved. This local candidate is awaiting manual testing.
