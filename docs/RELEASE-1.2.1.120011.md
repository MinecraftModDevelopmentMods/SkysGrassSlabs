# Sky's Grass Slabs 1.2.1 for Minecraft 1.20.1

Snow caps now follow higher snowy terraces and exposed cold ground. Slabs placed
beside snow can use their snowy appearance straight away.

Different crafting results stay on separate recipe-book buttons. Joining two
slabs also checks for entities in the space the full block would occupy.

Existing terrain stays unchanged, including saved chunks that have not finished
decoration. Newly generated chunks still receive suitable grass transitions.

The mod works on its own. Terrain Smoother and Building Pieces remain optional,
and existing block IDs, settings and saved progress are kept.
